First run

What setup asks for, and why.

Each permission is requested at the step that needs it, with an explanation of what it unlocks. You can decline any of them and still use the rest.

01

Talk to me

Microphone access, so you can speak instead of typing. It also shows you the shortcut that starts listening.

02

Reach me anywhere

Input monitoring, so the global shortcut works from whichever app you're in rather than only when lil' help is focused.

03

Click and type

Accessibility access, so it can act on your behalf in other applications instead of only describing what to do.

04

See the screen

Screen recording permission, for screen context and for demo recordings. A restart is sometimes required after granting it.

05

Use apps

Nothing up front. Access to each service is requested in the conversation that needs it, with a reason attached.

06

Connect your account

Sign in through the browser with a one-time code, or use API billing instead.

After that, a short tour of the bar.

If something breaks

The menu-bar icon stays available even when the bar is tucked away. It explains what failed and offers the one repair action that fixes it.

  • Restart the engine, or open the log
  • Jump to the right System Settings pane
  • See which permissions still work

Recovery, not a support ticket
